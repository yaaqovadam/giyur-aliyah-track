✅ Official Halachic Answer: Shabbat candles are lit 18 to 20 minutes before sunset, or 40 minutes before in Jerusalem, lighting at least two candles corresponding to Shamor and Zachor. According to Ashkenazi custom, a woman kindles the lights first and then covers her eyes while reciting the blessing ’Lehadlik Ner Shel Shabbat’ so that she does not formally accept Shabbat before performing the mitzvah, whereas Sephardic women generally recite the blessing prior to kindling.

✅ Official Halachic Answer: A woman customarily accepts Shabbat immediately upon lighting the candles unless she made an explicit condition (Tnai) beforehand that she is not yet accepting the day. A man accepts Shabbat in the synagogue when the congregation recites ’Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbat’ or the stanza of ’Boi VeShalom’ during Kabbalat Shabbat, or upon answering Barchu in Maariv if praying alone.
